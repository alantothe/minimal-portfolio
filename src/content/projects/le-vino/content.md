# Le Vino - Online Wine Marketplace

A sophisticated online wine marketplace platform designed for wine enthusiasts and collectors to discover, browse, and purchase curated wines from around the world.

## Features

- **Wine Discovery** - Browse and filter wines by region, vintage, and type
- **Detailed Listings** - Comprehensive product information with tasting notes
- **User Reviews** - Community-driven ratings and reviews
- **Secure Checkout** - Safe and reliable payment processing
- **Wishlist & Recommendations** - Personalized wine suggestions based on preferences

## Tech Stack

- Modern web technologies for seamless user experience
- Responsive design for mobile and desktop
- Secure payment integration
- Real-time inventory management

## Experience

A curated selection of wines from premium vineyards worldwide, making it easy to find the perfect wine for any occasion.
